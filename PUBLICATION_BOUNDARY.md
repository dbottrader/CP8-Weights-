## Removed—not withheld as project secrecy

Only the following classes are removed or replaced:

- unrelated personal conversations and tangents
- medical, family, custody, relationship, recovery, housing, and personal-financial details
- private addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, credentials, access tokens, private keys, and account identifiers
- private third-party information and material lacking redistribution permission
- duplicate exports and embedded binaries when a hash, manifest, or canonical artifact is already preserved

Mixed threads are handled message by message. A personal preamble may be replaced while later patent, trademark, code, token, or architecture material remains in the public project record.

## Distribution limitation

The connected Google Drive API for this consumer Gmail account cannot grant an anonymous “anyone with the link” permission. The full sanitized source archive is complete and hash-sealed, but its Drive visibility remains controlled by the project steward. This is a distribution-permission limitation, not an evidence or project-data omission.

## Evidence boundary

Historical dialogue remains `HISTORICAL_PROJECT_DIALOGUE_UNVERIFIED`. Inclusion in the corpus proves lineage and prior expression; it does not by itself prove an engineering, scientific, legal, or financial claim. Promotion still requires artifact-level tests, replay, reproduction, and witness records.

## Contribution rule

Contributors must not submit personal data, credentials, private conversations unrelated to the project, or copyrighted datasets without permission. Project submissions should be attributable, minimally sufficient, licensed, reproducible, and accompanied by hashes or receipts.
