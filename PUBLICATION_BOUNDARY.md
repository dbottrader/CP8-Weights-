# Public Release Boundary

## Public now

- model architecture and configuration
- training and verification scripts
- training-loss log
- model status and verification receipts
- SHA-256 manifests
- canonical governance instruction seed
- ecosystem documentation
- collaboration and independent-reproduction request

## Withheld pending review

- full OpenAI conversation export
- full Gemini and Kimi conversation content
- private messages and correspondence
- medical, family, custody, financial, and legal records
- addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, credentials, tokens, and private keys
- third-party material without confirmed redistribution rights
- the private 31 MB recovery training text and derived JSONL corpus

## Reason

A corpus can be technically useful while still being inappropriate to publish. The project requires human privacy, consent, copyright, and licensing review before any recovered conversation corpus is released or used for broader public training.

## Contribution rule

Contributors must not submit private conversations, personal data, credentials, copyrighted datasets without permission, or unverifiable claims. Submit minimal, attributable, reviewable artifacts with explicit licensing.
